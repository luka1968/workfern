Key Features

🖍️

Highlight Anywhere

Select and highlight text on any article, blog post, or webpage with a single click.

📝

Add Personal Context

Attach personal notes and thoughts to your highlights right on the page.

💎

Obsidian Native Integration

Export your highlights perfectly formatted for Obsidian. Includes "Copy to Obsidian" and "Copy Callout" formats.

Markdown Export

Download all your highlights from a page into a clean, well-structured .md file.

Persistent Storage

Your highlights are saved locally. When you revisit a page, your previous highlights will still be there.

Privacy First

We don't read your browsing history. We only process the text you explicitly choose to highlight.
