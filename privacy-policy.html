Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Feb 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Workfern ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclosure, and safeguard your information when you use our website www.workfern.com and our Chrome Extension (the "Service").

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you register on the Service, express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products, or otherwise contact us.

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, and contact data provided when you sign up or request support.
  • Payment Data: All payment data is stored by our third-party payment processor. We do not store your credit card numbers or banking information on our servers.
  • Extension Usage Data: Our Chrome Extension is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or sell your browsing history, search history, or website content. Most processing happens locally on your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect or receive:

  • To facilitate account creation and logon processes.
  • To send you administrative information (e.g., product updates, changes to terms).
  • To fulfill and manage your orders and subscriptions.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To enforce our terms, conditions, and policies.

4. Sharing Your Information

We only share information with the following third parties:

  • Payment Processors: We use a Merchant of Record to handle billing and payments securely.
  • Service Providers: We may share data with hosting services or email delivery services strictly for the purpose of operating our business.

5. GDPR and Data Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights, including:

  • The right to access, update, or delete the information we have on you.
  • The right to rectification.
  • The right to object.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at support@workfern.com.

6. Data Security

We have implemented appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process.
